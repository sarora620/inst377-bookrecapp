Rediscovering Myself, One Page at a Time

When I quit social media, I didn't know who I was anymore. I'd spent so much time curating a version of myself for the feed that I'd lost touch with the real me—the one who existed before the endless scrolling, the constant comparison, and the dopamine hits of notifications.

The hardest part wasn't giving up social media itself. It was the void it left behind, especially at night. I'd reach for my phone out of habit, ready to scroll until my eyes burned and my sleep schedule was ruined. But instead of installing the apps again, I picked up a book.

Just a few pages at first. Nothing ambitious—I wasn't trying to finish a novel in one sitting. I'd turn on my candle warmer, heat up a cinnamon bun, and read for 10, maybe 15 minutes before bed. That small ritual became my anchor. It kept me off my phone, protected my sleep, and slowly helped me remember what I actually enjoyed—not what the algorithm told me to enjoy.

"A reader lives a thousand lives before he dies. The man who never reads lives only one." — George R.R. Martin
